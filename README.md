# PocketPal

A tiny handheld pet you raise in your browser.

Feed it. Play with it. Watch it grow up. Train it. Battle friends with a code.
Your pal is yours — messy care and good care grow into different lives.

---

## Start

Open the game and tap the egg.

On your phone, use **Add to Home Screen** so it feels like an app
and care alerts can still reach you after you leave.

Your save stays on that phone or computer. Same browser, same pal.

Pet Memory has **5 slots**. Parked pals wait frozen. Only the active pal needs care.
They do not return home from old age. Neglect still can. Age is how long you have been together.

---

## The three buttons

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **A** | Move left. Cycle the icons. Guard in battle. |
| **B** | Confirm. Attack in battle. Hold B to pause. |
| **C** | Back. Cancel. |

On the pink shell:

- Bell — care alerts on or off
- Speaker — sound
- Palette — shell color

---

## A day with your pal

Keep the hearts up.

- Empty food hearts → feed
- Empty happy hearts → play
- Pile on the screen → clean
- Swirl / sick face → medicine
- Night → lights off so they can sleep. Set bed and wake in Status (A / B).
- A full night (6h+) keeps them rested. Short nights tire them and can steer a worse teen/adult. Ears and tail stay the same.

They age up in the morning after a night’s rest.
Ignore them and they get stubborn. Care for them and they shine.
The body they hatch with — ears, tail, markings — stays with that life as they evolve.
Train and breed to find missing Paldex looks. Care changes the body, not the species.

## Train and battle

Teen and older.

**Train (XP)** is a short timing game. Wait for GO, tap the letter.
That XP raises battle level and power.

**Training Battle** is a practice rival.
**Battle Friend** uses a 6-letter code.

In a fight you will see:

- **HP** — health this match
- **LV** — level from training
- **PWR** — power from stage, discipline, XP, and care
- **STA** — your stamina. Attack spends it. Guard gives +2 back.
- **CHG** — charge while you mash B on Attack

Read the stats, press **B** to lock in, then **A** to guard or **B** to attack.
If STA hits 0 you must guard.

## Friends, codes, breeding

Connect shows **your code**. It is 6 letters.

1. Send it to a friend.
2. They type yours under Add Friend.
3. Select them and tap **Battle Friend**, or **Breed** if both pals are adults.

Breeding writes the parent into Family Album (and Pet Memory if a slot is free).
The baby is a new generation with its own look.

---

## Paldex and Memory

**Paldex** is every species you have met — yours, stored pals, family, and rivals.
There are 256 looks. Yours is marked with a star. Use Prev / Next to flip pages.

**Pet Memory** holds 3 lives. Store the one you are raising, start a new egg,
or swap who is on the screen.

---

## The bell

Tap the bell so it rings. Allow notifications when the phone asks.

You get pings when they are hungry, sad, dirty, or sick —
even after you close PocketPal.

Tap the bell again to silence it. That stops the pings.

On iPhone: Add to Home Screen first, then turn the bell on.
On Android: Chrome + Allow notifications is enough.
